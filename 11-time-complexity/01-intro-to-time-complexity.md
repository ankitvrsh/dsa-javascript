# Introduction to Time Complexity

Time and Space Complexity Analysis / Algorithm Complexity Analysis:

- Efficiency of an algorithm in terms of time taken is important.
- Efficiency of an algorithm in terms of space/memory taken is important.

It is very important for us to understand how exactly algorithm’s efficiency is measured wrt how much time it is going to take and how much space it is going to take because these are one of the important metrics that exist out there.

## Measuring algorithms w.r.t. hardware

The judgement of the algorithm should be independent of the hardware that we are testing on.
